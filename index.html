User Guide & VR Controls

🥽 Meta Quest 3S VR Controller Controls

  • Laser Point & Trigger: Aim your controller laser raycaster at any furniture piece and pull the trigger to select it.
  • Grab & Drag: Hold the controller Trigger while pointing at furniture to drag it freely across the room floor in 3D space. Release trigger to place it. Haptic vibration confirms pickup and drop.
  • In-VR 3D Kiosk: A floating interactive kiosk is stationed in the room. Point your laser at any button on the kiosk (Add Bed/Sofa/Chair, Rotate, Clone, Delete, Wall Paint) and click trigger to design without removing your headset!
  • Thumbsticks: Push thumbstick left or right to rotate the active selected object in 45° steps.
  • Buttons (A / X): Quick-rotate selected furniture by 90°.
  • Buttons (B / Y): Duplicate or delete selected furniture.

🌐 Opening on Meta Quest 3S Headset

WebXR requires a Secure Context (https:// or localhost).
1. Local Network HTTPS / ngrok: Serve the folder via python -m http.server 8000 and forward via ngrok http 8000 to obtain a secure HTTPS URL.
2. Meta Quest Browser: In your Quest 3S headset, launch the Meta Quest Browser, open the HTTPS link, and click the "Enter VR" button!
3. The browser will enter full 6DoF immersive room-scale VR with your Touch Plus controllers active.

🖥️ Desktop Mouse & Keyboard Controls

  • Select Furniture: Click directly on any 3D furniture item with your mouse.
  • Drag Furniture: Click and drag furniture across the floor plane with your mouse. The camera look controls pause automatically during dragging for precision control.
  • Walk Around: Use W A S D keys to navigate inside the room.
  • Look Around: Click and drag on empty room space to rotate your camera view.
  • Rotate Furniture: Press R or click the "Rotate 90°" button.
  • Delete Furniture: Press Delete or Backspace.
  • Bird's Eye Mode: Click the "Bird's Eye View" button in the top bar to switch to an architectural dollhouse view! The ceiling hides automatically for an unobstructed top-down view.

✨ Implemented Core Features

  • Self-Contained: 100% contained in one single HTML file. No npm, no external model files, no build step required.
  • Zero-Dependency Procedural Textures: Parquet oak, dark walnut, light maple, Italian marble, terrazzo, polished concrete, and abstract wall art are generated dynamically via HTML5 canvas.
  • Procedural Sound & Haptics: Web Audio API synthesizes UI feedback clicks, placement thuds, and save chimes, with Quest controller haptic vibration.
  • 8 Rich Furniture Types: Bed, Sofa, Lounge Chair, Table, Wardrobe, Bookshelf, Floor Lamp (with real point light), and Indoor Plant.
  • Boundary Clamping: Keeps furniture strictly inside the 8m x 8m room boundaries.
  • Browser LocalStorage: Full persistence of furniture layout, wall colors, floor materials, and lighting presets.
